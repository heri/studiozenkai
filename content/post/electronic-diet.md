Towards the end of ["About Heri"](https://studiozenkai.com/post/about-heri/), I wrote about regular exercise.

Not jut for the body, for the mind too. Exercise is not just swweating or lifting weights. It's challenging yourself. Your whole body or mind screams "stop!" but somehow, you find the motivation to continue to the next hour. Then it continues.

<!--more-->

You win when you push past the point where others quit.

First is physical exercise. You can either do cardio, which is sustaining lighter physical activity for long periods of time, or inversely intensity training. I mix trail running, martial arts, weights, cold showers, hike and [serious handyman work](https://studiozenkai.com/post/lessons-from-vacation-property-airbnb/). Physical exercise not only make you look good, it brings a range of proven health and mental benefits. On the other hand, lack of physical exercise is a guarantee for early physical ailments and shorter life expectancy.

Second form is diet. Eating less. Eating clean. [Or eating at set times](https://dailyhealthpoints.wordpress.com/2017/01/07/improving-metabolism-with-intermittent-fasting/) -- or not all. In 2015, I did not eat any type of artificial sugar or processed foods. It was a challenge ! I have friends who stopped drinking alcohol for months. Every 3 months, I do not eat for a whole weekend. Why? Because in many ways, modern life encourages us towards over-eating, and also pushes us towards ultra processed foods, like geese at a foie gras factory. Severly cutting your diet is a way to discover your dependency towards this system, and also to affirm, you control what you eat. There are also multiple proven health benefits.

Third form of exercise is what I call ["financial diet"](https://studiozenkai.com/post/nobuy_challenge/). Once a year, I pledge not to spend anything for a month. Forget Amazon purchases, streaming subscriptions, coffee shop runs, ski trips or getting a treat for the kids. Just essentials: food, basic bills and taxes. This can be hard socialy. How do you tell friends you cannot have lunch with them? What if a family member needs something? 😅 But you learn. Most expenses aren’t needs. They’re habits. And bad ones at that.

In January, I will a fourth form of exercise: electronic diet. No devices except for work. No Netflix. No YouTube. No online payments. No scrolling the endless scroll. I will go outside more, [read more books](https://studiozenkai.com/post/habits-2025/), and play more games with the kids. [Move more](https://studiozenkai.com/post/habits-2025/). Think more. Write more. Breaking free from the array of electronic devices controlling our physical and mental life. 

It’s not about rejecting the modern world. It’s about proving I can live without it if I choose. It is also building resilience. Because what you have today might be absent tomorrow, temporarily or permanently, and you don't want to be caught like a deer in headlights.

I know though people will see all this as meaningless, unusual and stupid. Isn't it like starving a junkie?
