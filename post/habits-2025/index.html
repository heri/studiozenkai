4 Habits to pick up in 2025

Tue, Dec 3, 2024

2024 is ending and I have decided on 4 habits to pick up in 2025:

  • Having fun with programming again. I went from having fun in hackathons and working on one-off creative concepts to write boring pull requests for huge organizations. Day to day involve politics, outdated processes and blasé coworkers. If having fun in a job is not possible anymore, I want to be able to create and just do fun stuff again in 2025, even if it is silly concepts.
  • Reading When I was a teenager, I read a book every two days. The Internet captivated me and shortened my attention. I want to be able to loose myself again in deep fiction, science works or even DIY books.
  • Community . Years ago, it was all about community, either college, montréal, arts or other. The pandemic came and put an end to all of that. But I believe communities are now more than ever necessary. I’ll put myself out there and provide ways to connect with each other. Does it mean monthly events? Or online connections? 🤷 We’ll see
  • Fixing, Making, Growing sustainably I started having manual tools with the chalet, first poking at stuff, screwing around, then seriously repairing or putting docks together. The next step for me would be to actually make stuff and also grow stuff. I will take the same attitude as programming above, which is : it should be fun and it should not feel like work

Additionally, here are three other goals I’ll put some time to. No biggie if I can’t make it though!

  • Cooking I am able to put things together in the kitchen but do not have a signature dish or anything that gets any praise. So I will look to learn a few handful mains/desert/sandwiches recipes that the young and not so young ones will love. Bonus points if it is simple and sustainable.
  • Winter Sports I’ve been in Canada for almost half my life and I’ve never done any skiing or snowboarding here. I am not looking to get back the level I had in Europe but will see into going back gently with kids.
  • Camping Like skiing, camping is something I loved doing back in Europe but never got around doing anything in Canada, perhaps due to local laws. Nevertheless, we have the gear and look forward to camp in 2025, beyond renting a Park Canada A-Frame cabin, which is cheating.
  • Climbing If I can, I will take a few classes and a few gym sessions, and perhaps go explore mountains with the kids
