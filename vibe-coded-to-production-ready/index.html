Three ways a weekend app lets you down

It works on your MacBook

Questions

Will you tell me to throw it away and start over?

Rarely. In my experience, most AI-built apps have a sound core and a missing layer around it. I'll only recommend a rebuild if fixing would cost more, and I'll show you the numbers.

I'm not technical. Will I understand the report?

Yes. It's written for the person who owns the risk, not for engineers. Each finding says what could happen, how likely it is, and what fixing it costs.

What tools and languages do you work with?

I work on apps built with Lovable, Bolt, Replit, v0, Cursor, Claude Code and GitHub Copilot, in Python, TypeScript, Elixir and Ruby, with the usual databases and cloud hosts. If I'm not the right fit, I'll say so on the first call.

You're one person. What happens when you're away?

You work directly with me: no account managers, and no juniors learning on your app. I work Monday to Friday, Eastern time, and I tell you about time off weeks in advance. I won't pick up the phone on December 25, so part of my job is making sure you never need me to: alerts, a written runbook, a tested rollback, and documentation good enough that another engineer could take over tomorrow. That's the last item on the checklist, and I hold my own work to it.
